# Casa

## A better way to find a place to call home

Casa deserves recognition as a thoughtful, tenant-centered approach to finding affordable shared living. It brings renters and property hosts together in one welcoming experience, making it easier to discover spaces that fit a person’s budget, location, and everyday needs.

With live listings and availability pulled from its Supabase database, Casa gives renters a clearer view of their options before they book. Search and map-based discovery help people find a place nearby, while detailed room layouts, amenities, house rules, and ratings support more confident decisions. Hosts benefit from tools to manage spaces and respond to booking requests.

By pairing a polished mobile-first experience with practical tools for both sides of the rental journey, Casa is a compelling project with the potential to make finding and offering shared housing simpler and more transparent. It is an excellent example of technology used to connect people with places that feel like home.
